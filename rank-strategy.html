JEE MAIN EXECUTION PLAN

01 · SPEED

Train fast recognition, short calculations and disciplined question selection. Do not spend disproportionate time on one problem.

02 · ACCURACY

Every avoidable error is expensive. Review incorrect and guessed questions after every test.

03 · MOCK DISCIPLINE

Use full-length tests to measure endurance, time management, attempt selection and consistency.

04 · NCERT CONTROL

For Chemistry especially, maintain systematic NCERT-based revision where applicable instead of relying only on problem solving.

JEE ADVANCED EXECUTION PLAN

01 · DEPTH

Prioritise multi-concept problems and learn why a method works, not just which formula produces an answer.

02 · QUESTION SELECTION

Practice deciding which problems deserve immediate attention, which require deeper work and which should be temporarily skipped.

03 · PARTIAL MARKING AWARENESS

Understand the marking scheme of each test and practise extracting maximum marks without reckless attempts.

04 · STAMINA

Build the ability to maintain high-quality reasoning throughout long, difficult examination sessions.

03

04
